Cost of capital

Where the value comes from: capital in place and economic profit

The base case's enterprise value, split two ways. Capital in place is the invested capital on the balance sheet. Economic profit is after-tax operating profit less a charge, at the cost of capital, for the capital in use. Free cash flow can be negative while economic profit is positive: the company is spending on growth that earns more than it costs.
